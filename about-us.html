Introduction

Shree Secondary School, Rajhena is a public academic institute located in Rajhena, Dang district of Nepal. This school is affiliated with the (NEB) National Examinations Board and approved by Ministry of Education (MoE), Nepal. It offers Ten plus Two (10+2) programs in Humanities, Management, and Education stream.

Shree Secondary School, Rajhena, Dang provides the basic to Ten Plus Two (10+2) level education with moderate fees structures. It provides scholarships schemes for deserving, hardworking and the poor students of the society. Shree Secondary School Rajhena, Dang provides various facilities such as extracurricular activities (ECA), Educational Tour, Conference, Sports, Counseling, Classrooms, Library, and scholarships facilities.

    A Tradition for Excellence

    Accreditation

    The school implements the National Curriculum. At the Class 10 stage, students are prepared for the School Education Examination(S. E. E) English, Nepali, Maths, Science, Social Studies, Environment, Population & Health (E. P. H.) and Optional Maths are compulsory for all students. Students have to choose either Computer Studies or Accountancy as their Optional Subject.

    Students of the school have done very well in the board examinations.

    Every year more than 150 students appear for the S. E E. exams. The success rate is about 95% out of which 80% secure the first division.

    The Higher Secondary Wing of the school is affiliated to the Higher Secondary Education Council, Sanothimi, Bhaktapur, Nepal. The following streams are offered at the Higher Secondary Level:

    1. Science
    2. Commerce and Management
    3. Education

    At this level, English and Nepali are compulsory. In the Science group, Physics, Chemistry, Biology and Maths are offered. In the Commerce group, Economics, Business Organization & Office Management and Accountancy are offered. In higher education, its aim is to maintain a first-class, cost-effective system which helps to satisfy the social needs and to offer an opportunity to students to enhance their knowledge.

    The Rajhena technical wing is affiliated to the CTEVT of Nepal; Offering Diploma and Pre-diploma level courses at at very cost-effective level.
    The following streams are offered at the Higher Secondary Level:

  • Pre-Diploma in Civil Engineering (Civil Sub-Overseer) (Civil Sub-Overseer)
  • Diploma in Civil Engineering
  • Diploma in Geomatics Engineering
  • Diploma in Agriculture (Plant Science)
