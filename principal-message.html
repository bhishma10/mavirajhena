MESSAGE FROM Principal

As the principal of Shree Secondary School, I am honored to extend a warm welcome to all students, parents, teachers, and staff as we embark on another academic year filled with promise and potential. At Shree Secondary School, we uphold a steadfast commitment to providing a nurturing and inclusive learning environment where every individual is encouraged to flourish and achieve their utmost potential. Our dedicated team of educators is deeply committed to fostering academic excellence, nurturing personal growth, and instilling values of integrity, respect, and resilience in our students.

Since we are living in an age of communication, the school has an effective system of communication for the proper dissemination of information. The school uses the latest communication tools to keep everyone informed about the activities of the school.
As we navigate the challenges and opportunities that lie ahead, I urge each member of our school community to embrace a spirit of collaboration, curiosity, and compassion. Together, let us strive to create a vibrant educational community where every voice is heard, every talent is celebrated, and every dream is supported. With determination and dedication, I am confident that we will continue to cultivate a culture of excellence and empower our students to become responsible global citizens who make meaningful contributions to society. Thank you for entrusting us with the privilege of guiding and shaping the future leaders of tomorrow.

 

Mr. Uddhab Neupane

Shree Secondary School
