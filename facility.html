Facilities

  • Sports

    At Shree Secondary School, sports are integral to our ethos, promoting physical fitness, teamwork, and character development among students. With a range of athletic opportunities and dedicated coaching, we nurture a culture of excellence and sportsmanship. School provides diverse sports facilities to the students with necessary infrastructure and amenities.

  • Science Laboratories

    At Shree Secondary School, our science laboratory stands as a cornerstone of experiential learning, igniting curiosity and fostering a deep appreciation for scientific inquiry. Equipped with modern amenities and cutting-edge equipment, our lab provides students with a dynamic environment to explore theoretical concepts through hands-on experimentation. Under the guidance of experienced educators, students engage in practical demonstrations, conduct research, and collaborate on projects, enabling them to develop critical thinking skills and apply theoretical knowledge to real-world scenarios. Through this immersive experience, we aim to cultivate a new generation of innovative thinkers and problem solvers prepared to excel in the ever-evolving field of science.

  • Computer Laboratories

    At Rajhena School, our commitment to technological advancement is reflected in our state-of-the-art computer and ICT (Information and Communication Technology) lab. Equipped with the latest hardware, software, and high-speed internet connectivity, our lab serves as a hub for innovation and digital literacy. Here, students are provided with hands-on experience in coding, multimedia design, and software development, preparing them for the digital age and future careers in technology.
    Furthermore, our dedicated team of faculties ensures that students receive comprehensive training and support to harness the full potential of these resources. Through interactive lessons and collaborative projects, students not only enhance their technical skills but also develop critical thinking, problem-solving, and communication abilities. At Rajhena School, we are committed to providing a dynamic learning environment where students thrive as confident and proficient users of technology, poised to excel in an increasingly interconnected world.

  • Canteen

    At the canteen, food is sold on a daily payment basis. Besides serving lunch, the canteen also serves cooked food such as noodles and snacks. Soft drinks, tea, coffee and packaged food items are also available in the canteen. The school ensures that all food items conform to acceptable standards.
