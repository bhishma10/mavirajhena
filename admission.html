ADMISSION PROCEDURE

Discover our straightforward admission process, ensuring ease and clarity for prospective students and their families. From application submission to enrollment, we're here to guide you every step of the way.The Official Application Form must be completed and registered by paying the registration fee. However, registration does not guarantee admission to school. Registration is followed by an Entrance Examination and an interview.

To be eligible for admission the child’s age should be

Age Class
2+ years For Play Group Montessori
3+ years For Nursery(English Medium And Montessori)
4+ years For Class Upper KG(English Medium And Montessori)
5+ years For Class 1 and so on.
Step I:

Complete the registration form and attach photocopies of all relevant documents. Submit your application form to the Admissions Office within the registrationn deadline.

Step II:

Registration is followed by an Entrance Examination and an interview.

Step III:

The entrance result will be published in notice board.

Step IV:

Selected students must complete the admission formalities very soon because no registration will be taken after fulfilling the students quota.

Step V:

Pay admission fee, the annual charges, the school fee and other fees.

Note:

In lower classes, admissions are sometimes taken in the middle of a session. This depends on the number of seats available.

Scholorship

At our school, we believe in recognizing and nurturing talent, irrespective of financial barriers. Through our scholarship programs, we aim to empower deserving students by providing them with access to quality education. From academic excellence to extracurricular achievements, we offer a range of scholarships to celebrate and support the diverse talents and accomplishments of our students.

Our scholarship initiatives are designed to foster a culture of meritocracy and inclusivity, ensuring that every student has the opportunity to reach their full potential. Through generous contributions from donors, alumni, and community partners, we are able to offer financial assistance to students who demonstrate exceptional promise and dedication to their academic and personal development.

Whether it's through need-based scholarships, merit-based awards, or specialized grants, we are committed to creating pathways for students to pursue their dreams and aspirations. We encourage students to explore our scholarship opportunities and embark on a transformative educational journey with us.

Examination

The school academic year is divided into three terms each divided into 4 months.

Midterm exams serve as checkpoints for students to assess their understanding and progress in the curriculum, providing valuable feedback for both students and teachers. These assessments offer an opportunity for students to reflect on their learning and make adjustments for continued academic growth.

Terminal examinations are held at the end of each term. There are three rounds of terminal exams. Each exams carries 100 marks. These exams cover the lessons taught

Our test reports provide a comprehensive overview of your child's academic performance, offering valuable insights into strengths and areas for improvement. Through regular communication and collaboration, we strive to empower parents in supporting their child's educational journey

All the examinations (except Nepali & Social Studies & Local curriculam) are taken in their respective teaching medium. Besides short tests and terminal examinations, a regular assessment of a student's performance is made on a day-to-day basis.

Tests on subsidiary subjects such as handwriting, spelling, science & computer practicals, speaking & listening, arts & crafts etc. are taken before tests on the major subjects are conducted.

A student is promoted to the immediate higher classes or is allowed to take the Board exams only if his/her performance is satisfactory in all the exams conducted in an academic session. The final result is prepared on the basis of the marks obtained in the mid-term tests, terminal exams and Discipline.

Fees Rules

  • Admission Fee is paid at the time of Admission only. It is charged only once during a student’s association with the school. This fee is charged to a new student at the time of admission, this fee is non-refundable.
  • All fees are charged for the full session.
  • There shall be no refund of fees under any circumstances.
  • Parents wishing to withdraw their wards from the school bus facility in the middle of the year will have to pay the bus fee for the whole year.
  • There shall be no refund of any fees (Not even when a student is suspended or expelled from the school).
  • In case a student is asked to leave in the middle of a session due to misconduct or any other reason, he/she will have to pay the charges for the whole academic session.
Note:
  • The school provides bus facility to the students on request.
  • Buses ply on definite routes. Bus routes cannot be changed on request.
  • The Examination Fee includes only the school examination fee.
  • For solo performances in sports, music etc. the students are expected to bring their own equipment.
  • The materials required for projects, exhibitions and fairs are to be brought by the students.
  • Students are charged for report cards, fee cards, calendars, I. D. cards, diaries & prospectus etc.
